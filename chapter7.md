CHAPTER 7: THE BREACH

The door shook again.

Harder.

A voice followed.

Not human.

“Open… access… required…”

Simon froze. “That’s corrupted speech synthesis.”

John didn’t respond.

Another impact.

The lock began to fail.

Simon’s fingers moved fast across his system interface.

Then—

“John,” Simon said slowly. “We’re not dealing with a malfunction.”

A final impact shattered the door.
