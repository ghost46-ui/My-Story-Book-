CHAPTER 1: THE NORMAL LIFE

Simon lived like any other man on the surface.

Every morning followed the same pattern—predictable, clean, almost mechanical in its calmness.

He woke up at the same time every day without an alarm. His body had been trained for discipline long before comfort replaced danger. A short fitness routine in the living room—push-ups, controlled breathing, stretching—just enough to keep his instincts from sleeping completely.

Then coffee.

Always black. No sugar. No distractions.

After that, he would sit in silence at his desk inside his apartment. The city outside was alive, but for Simon, everything was distant. His work was remote—coded systems, security patches, quiet digital tasks that rarely demanded urgency.

He barely ever left home unless it was absolutely necessary.

A month passed like this.

Same cycle. Same silence. Same life pretending to be stable.

But stability, Simon knew, was often just delay before impact.
