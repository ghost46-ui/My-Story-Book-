CHAPTER 3: THE SILENCE ONLINE

By night, Simon had already started digging.

Every major news outlet repeated the same sentence like a programmed loop.

No leaks.

No government statement beyond the announcement.

No insider posts.

Even independent forums that usually thrived on chaos were strangely quiet.

That silence was unnatural.

Digital systems never went completely silent unless something forced them to.

Simon felt it then—a shift in pattern recognition he had developed years ago.

Something wasn’t just hidden.

Something was actively being erased.

He kept searching through the night.

Hours passed.

Then days.

Work disappeared from his mind entirely.
