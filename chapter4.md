CHAPTER 4: THE CALL FROM JOHN

On the second day, his phone rang.

“Simon,” a familiar voice said. “What the hell are you doing?”

It was John.

His former commander. His boss. His brother in everything but blood.

Simon exhaled slowly. “Something’s wrong.”

A pause.

“You’re saying that like you know it,” John replied.

“I do,” Simon said. “But I can’t prove it yet.”

John sighed. “Your files are incomplete. You’ve been offline from team communication. People are asking questions.”

“I don’t care about files,” Simon cut in. “Look at the city.”

“Or maybe you need rest,” John said more sharply now. “You’re overthinking this.”

Simon’s voice dropped.

“I’m not overthinking. I’m remembering how governments behave when they don’t want people to ask questions.”

Silence.

Then John asked quietly, “Where are you?”

“My apartment.”

“I’m coming.”
