CHAPTER 8: THE INFECTED

A figure stood outside.

Once human.

Now distorted.

Its body moved like it was being remotely corrected every second. Eyes flickered with interference patterns. Skin twitching unnaturally as if reality itself was struggling to render it properly.

Behind it—

More shapes.

John fired instantly.

One dropped—but didn’t behave like a normal body. It tried to stand again, as if the concept of falling wasn’t recognized.

Simon grabbed John. “Move!”

They ran.
