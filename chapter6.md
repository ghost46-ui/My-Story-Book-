CHAPTER 6: THE FIRST GLITCH

That evening, they decided to observe the curfew cycle together.

At 5:45 PM, the city began shutting down early.

At 6 PM sharp—

Everything changed.

Screens flickered.

Digital noise spiked.

Security cameras briefly distorted.

John noticed it first. “That wasn’t normal interference.”

Simon was already analyzing signals.

Then came the sound.

Faint.

Metallic.

Dragging.

Inside the building.

John reached for his weapon.

Simon checked the hallway feed.

Empty.

But the sound grew louder.

Step… drag… step…

John whispered, “That’s inside the corridor.”

Lights outside flickered violently.

Then went out.

And something heavy struck the door.
