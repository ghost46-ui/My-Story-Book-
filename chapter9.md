CHAPTER 9: THE FALLING CITY

Outside the apartment, the building had changed.

Hallways were no longer empty.

People walked—but not normally.

Some repeated loops.

Some stood frozen.

Some followed invisible instructions only they could see.

The city had stopped behaving like a place.

It was behaving like a system.

Controlled.

Corrected.

Rewritten.

Drones moved silently above.

Streetlights pulsed in rhythm.

And somewhere in the distance, a tower glowed red like a heartbeat.

John looked at Simon. “That’s it.”

Simon nodded.

“That’s the source.”
