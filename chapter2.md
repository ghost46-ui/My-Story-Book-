CHAPTER 2: THE ANNOUNCEMENT

It happened during a regular afternoon.

Simon was sitting on his couch, half-watching the news while scrolling through his laptop.

Then the screen changed.

A red banner flashed violently across every channel.

BREAKING NEWS

The anchor’s face looked unusually stiff. Not panicked. Not emotional. Controlled.

“The City will go under curfew from today. Citizens are requested to remain indoors after 6 PM.”

No explanation followed.

No reason.

No emergency briefing.

Just silence after the message repeated itself twice.

The broadcast ended.

For a few seconds, the world didn’t react.

Then confusion spread.

Social media exploded instantly—people joking, arguing, mocking it, calling it a test or propaganda stunt. The internet behaved like it always did when it didn’t understand something: it turned it into entertainment.

But Simon didn’t move.

He replayed the message in his mind.

Something was missing.

Not what was said—but what wasn’t.
